:::danger[This wipes your VPS!]
Reimaging completely erases everything on your VPS and replaces it with a fresh install of the operating system you choose. Take a backup of anything important first.
:::

## Available options

* **Ubuntu**
* **Debian**
* **AlmaLinux**
* **Rocky Linux**
* **Fedora**
* **FreeBSD**
* **Arch Linux**
* **openSUSE**

:::info[Windows]
Windows is also available, but requires opening a ticket with us first as there's an additional charge.
:::

:::info[Don't see what you need?]
You can also open a ticket to request a custom image. Whether we can support it depends on the image itself, we vet custom images to protect against things like malware.
:::

## Reimaging your VPS

### Locate your VPS \[step]

Navigate to the panel and click "Manage" on the VPS you'd like to reimage.

### Open the rebuild options \[step]

Scroll down to the VPS management options and click "Rebuild".

### Configure the rebuild \[step]

* Set the name and timezone you'd like to use.
* Select the operating system and version you want.

### 🎉 Done \[step]

Confirm the rebuild and wait for the install email, this contains your new IP address and login details, [see our first time setup guide](/vps/first-setup) if you need a refresher on logging in.
