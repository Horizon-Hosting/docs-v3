You're free to cancel your service(s) at any time. Any refund requests **must** be made via a billing ticket, or they will not be handled.

:::tip[Questions?]
If you have any questions or need support, [contact us](/general/contact-us).
:::

## Cancelling a Horizon service

### Open the billing panel \[step]

Navigate to the [Billing Panel](https://hrzn.link/billing) and log in.

### Manage the service \[step]

On your client dashboard, click "Manage" on the service you'd like to cancel.

### Request cancellation \[step]

On the product details page, choose "Request Cancellation".

### Fill in the details \[step]

* **Cancellation Reason** helps us understand why you're leaving so we can improve, this is optional but appreciated.
* **Cancellation Type**:
  * **Immediate**: processed within 24 hours, your server is removed straight away.
  * **End of Billing Cycle**: your server is kept until your next payment is due, then removed.

Click "Request Cancellation" to submit. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's processed.

## Cancelling a PayPal subscription

If you pay via PayPal pre-approved payments (subscription), cancelling your Horizon service does **not** automatically cancel the PayPal side, you'll need to cancel that separately.

### Log in to PayPal \[step]

Navigate to [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com) and log in.

### Open account settings \[step]

Hover over your name in the top right corner, and click "Account Settings".

### Manage pre-approved payments \[step]

Choose "Money, banks and cards" from the sidebar, scroll to the bottom of the page, and click "Manage pre-approved payments".

### Cancel the payment \[step]

Select the pre-approved payment for Horizon Hosting, then click "Cancel".
