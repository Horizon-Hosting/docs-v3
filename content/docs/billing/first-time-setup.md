## Check your inbox \[step]

After ordering, you'll receive a couple of automated emails:

* A **billing account** email, confirming your account on our [Billing Panel](https://hrzn.link/billing).
* A separate email (or emails) containing your **game panel** login for each service you've ordered, this is the panel you'll use day-to-day to manage your server, files, and databases.

:::info[Didn't get an email?]
Check your spam/junk folder first. If you still can't find it after 10-20 minutes, [contact us](/general/contact-us).
:::

## Log in to the billing panel \[step]

Use the [Billing Panel](https://hrzn.link/billing) to manage your orders, invoices, and support tickets.

## Log in to the game panel \[step]

Use the credentials from your game panel email to log in and start setting up your service, see the relevant "Getting Started" guide for your service under [Games](/games) or [VPS](/vps).

## Secure your account \[step]

We recommend changing your password on both panels, and enabling any available two-factor authentication, shortly after your first login.
