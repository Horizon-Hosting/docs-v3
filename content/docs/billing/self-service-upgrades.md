---
title: Self Service Upgrades
description: How to upgrade or downgrade your plan's resources from the billing panel.
type: How To
---

Need more RAM, storage, or player slots? You can change your plan yourself, without needing to open a ticket.

## Open the product \[step]

Log in to the [Billing Panel](https://hrzn.link/billing), go to your client dashboard, and "Manage" the service you want to change.

## Choose upgrade/downgrade \[step]

Select the "Upgrade/Downgrade" option on the product details page.

## Pick your new plan \[step]

Choose the new plan or add-on resources you'd like. Any price difference is prorated for the remainder of your current billing cycle.

## Confirm \[step]

Confirm the change and complete payment if required.

:::info[Some changes need a restart]
Resource changes (like RAM or disk) are usually applied automatically, but may require your service to be restarted before the new limits take effect.
:::

:::warning[Downgrades]
If you're downgrading and your service is currently using more resources than the new plan allows (e.g. more disk space than the new limit), you may need to free up space first.
:::
