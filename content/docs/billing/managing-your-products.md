## Open your product list \[step]

Log in to the [Billing Panel](https://hrzn.link/billing) and navigate to your client dashboard, this lists every active service on your account.

## Manage a service \[step]

Click "Manage" on the service you want to view. From here you can:

* View its status, next due date, and billing amount
* Access its game panel directly (if applicable)
* Open a support ticket relating to that specific service
* Request an upgrade, downgrade, or cancellation

:::tip[Server-specific issues]
For hands-on help with a specific server (not billing), our [Discord community support](/general/contact-us#community-support-channel) is usually faster than a billing ticket.
:::

## Update your details \[step]

Your contact details, payment methods, and invoices are also managed from the client dashboard, under "Account Details" and "Billing".
