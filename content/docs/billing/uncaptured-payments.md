---
title: Uncaptured Payments
description: What to do if you've been charged but didn't receive your service.
type: How To
---

An uncaptured payment is when a payment has gone through on your end, but the matching service was never provisioned (or a renewal payment didn't actually renew/unsuspend your service). This is usually caused by a processing error between your payment method and our billing system.

## Open a ticket \[step]

Head to the [Billing Panel](https://hrzn.link/billing) and open a support ticket, this isn't something we can resolve automatically, our team needs to verify the payment manually.

## Include the following details \[step]

To help us process this as quickly as possible, please include:

* **Proof of payment**: a receipt, bank/card statement line, or transaction ID showing the payment was taken
* **Location**: which region you expected the service to be in
* **Service**: which product/plan you purchased (or were renewing)
* **Any add-ons**: extra storage, backups, extra player slots, or any other extras included in the purchase

## What happens next \[step]

Once we've verified the payment, we'll either:

* **Manually process it**: provisioning (or restoring) your service with the details you provided, or
* **Refund you**: if we're unable to provision the service for any reason

:::tip[Refund timing]
If a refund is issued, it's processed automatically on our end, but it can take **up to 7 days** to appear back on your original payment method, depending on your bank or payment provider.
:::
