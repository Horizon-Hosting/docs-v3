---

:::tip[Pro Tip]
A GSLT can only be used on one server at a time. Make sure you don't share it across multiple servers!
:::

## Visit Steam's Website [step]
Visit the [Game Server Account Management page](https://steamcommunity.com/dev/managegameservers) and login using a steam account that meets the requirements listed on their page.

## Create a game server account [step]
Scroll to the bottom of the page and fill in the form as follows:
- App ID: 4000
- Memo: A friendly name for your server

After you have entered the details, click "Create" and copy the token from the "Login Token" column.

## 🎉 Done [step]
You now have a valid GSLT you can use for your Garry's Mod Service.
You can enter this into the startup section of your service under the "Steam Account Token" box.
