---
title: Installing Simple Voice Chat (Mod)
description: How to install the Simple Voice Chat mod on your Minecraft server.
type: How To
---

Simple Voice Chat is also available as a Fabric/Forge **mod**, for servers running mod loaders instead of Paper/Spigot. The setup is identical to [the plugin version](/games/minecraft/plugins/simple-voice-chat), with two differences:

* Download the **Fabric** or **Forge** build (matching your server's mod loader) from the [Simple Voice Chat download page](https://modrinth.com/plugin/simple-voice-chat/versions), instead of the Paper/Spigot build.
* Upload the `.jar` file to your `mods` folder, instead of `plugins`.

Once uploaded, follow the rest of [the plugin guide](/games/minecraft/plugins/simple-voice-chat#give-voice-chat-its-own-port) from the "Give voice chat its own port" step onwards, the configuration file and steps are the same.
