---
title: Changing the port on a game server
description: How to add, remove, and set a primary port for your game service
type: How-To
---

:::warning[Availability Notice]
Extra allocations are not available as a feature for every service. Please note that they may also be limited to the plan you are using.
:::

## Navigate to your service [step]
Go to [the game panel](https://panel.hrznhosting.com/) and open your service to its console view.

## Navigate to the network tab [step]
Using the sidebar, navigate to the tab called "Network" by clicking on it.

## Create a new allocation [step]
Click the "Create Allocation" button.

## Set the new allocation the primary allocation [step]
Use the "Make Primary" button to set the new allocation as the primary choice.

## 🎉 Done! [step]
You have now changed your default allocation on your service. You may view the new allocation at any time from the address card on the console view.

*If you are not planning on using the old allocation, we recommend removing it so you hae more allocations in your allowance to use.*
