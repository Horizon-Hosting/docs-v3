Getting further support is easy, just look at the available support options below.

:::tip[Billing & Refunds]
Billing enquiries and refund requests should be handled via our [Billing Panel Tickets](https://hrzn.link/billing), not Discord.
:::

## Discord

Join the [HRZN Discord](https://hrzn.link/discord), a friendly community full of people who are ready to help!

:::warning[Support Rules]
The Discord server rules must be followed at all times. Please note that we do have specific rules for both tickets and community support individually.
:::

### Community support channel

The `#community-support` channel is for addon or game-related questions, where fellow clients or our support team can lend a hand. For example, asking how to install [DarkRP](/games/garrys-mod/gamemodes/darkrp) or [Cobalt Essentials](/games/beammp/resources/ce-and-cei/installing-ce).

### Discord tickets

Discord tickets should be used for service-specific issues, where you think one of our team needs to step in.

To open a ticket, go to the `#new-ticket` channel and select the correct department, then sit back while one of our team comes to assist.

## Billing Panel Tickets

Use the [Billing Panel](https://hrzn.link/billing) for anything related to your account, payments, or service plan, including refund requests.
