## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to site owners. Cookies set by the website operator are called "first-party cookies". Cookies set by third parties (other than the site you are visiting) are called "third-party cookies".

Similar technologies include local storage and session storage, which work in a comparable way and are subject to the same consent rules under this policy.

## 2. How We Obtain Consent

When you first visit the Services, a cookie consent banner is displayed. We will not set any non-essential cookies until you have:

**(a)** clicked **Accept all**; or

**(b)** clicked **Manage preferences** and saved a choice that includes one or more optional categories.

Clicking **Reject optional** or closing the banner without making a selection will result in only strictly necessary cookies being set.

Your consent is stored in a cookie named `hrzn_consent` on `.hrznhosting.com` and is valid for 12 months. Because this cookie is scoped to `.hrznhosting.com`, a choice made on [hrznhosting.com](http://hrznhosting.com) applies automatically to [billing.hrznhosting.com](http://billing.hrznhosting.com) and vice versa — you will not be asked to consent twice across those two pages. This cookie does not, and cannot, extend to any other domain we operate, including [horizonnetworks.uk](http://horizonnetworks.uk), hrzn.link or [hrzn.gg](http://hrzn.gg).

You may change or withdraw your consent at any time by clicking **Cookie settings** in the footer of any page.

## 3. Do Not Track

Some web browsers transmit "Do Not Track" ("DNT") signals to websites. We do not alter our cookie or tracking practices in response to DNT signals. There is currently no consistent industry standard for honouring them, and no requirement under UK law — including the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) — that we do so.

If you wish to limit or opt out of non-essential cookies, please use our consent system described in Section 2 above. You can access it at any time by clicking **Cookie settings** in the footer of any page.

## 4. Cookies We Use

### 4.1 Strictly Necessary

These cookies are essential for the Services to function and cannot be switched off. They do not require your consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, as amended ("PECR"). They are set in response to actions you take, such as logging in or processing a payment.

| Cookie | Set By | Purpose | Expiry |
|----|----|----|----|
| `WHMCS*` | Horizon Hosting (WHMCS) | Authenticated session token. Keeps you logged in to the billing portal. The cookie name contains a unique suffix specific to our installation. | Session |
| `cf_clearance` | Cloudflare | Confirms your browser passed the Cloudflare bot and DDoS challenge. Required to access the site. | 1 year |
| `__stripe_mid` | Stripe | Device fingerprint used by Stripe for fraud prevention. Set on payment pages only. | 1 year |
| `__stripe_sid` | Stripe | Short-lived payment session token used during checkout. | \~30 minutes |
| `hrzn_consent` | Horizon Hosting | Stores your cookie consent preferences so you are not asked again. | 1 year |
| `g_state` | Google | Supports the Sign-In with Google option on the billing portal login page, which you may use instead of an email address and password. | Session |

### 4.2 Analytics

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the Services, which pages are visited most, and where errors occur. All data collected is anonymised and aggregated. No information is used to identify individual users, and no data is shared with advertisers.

| Cookie | Set By | Purpose | Expiry |
|----|----|----|----|
| `ph_phc_*_posthog` | PostHog | Anonymous session and event tracking. Records page views, feature usage and errors. Hosted in the EU. | 1 year |
| `posthog_ph_id` | PostHog | Anonymous device identifier used to link browsing sessions over time without identifying you personally. | 2 hours |

Analytics cookies are only set after you have given your consent. If you decline or withdraw consent, PostHog will be instructed to stop capturing data for your session.

PECR, as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, introduced a narrow exemption for cookies used solely for statistical purposes to improve a website. Because `posthog_ph_id` links your device across separate browsing sessions, we do not rely on this exemption and continue to treat all PostHog cookies as requiring your consent.
