## 2. Scope of Support

We aim to assist with issues relating to the core infrastructure and Services we provide. Support requests should relate to your use of, or a fault with, the Services themselves. The scope of support we provide varies depending on the type of Service you use, as set out below.

### 2.1 Managed Services

Some Services are marketed and provided as a specific, pre-configured software product hosted by us (for example, a game server product). For these Services, we will generally provide support to the best of our ability in relation to: billing; the underlying hardware; any control panel we provide; software we have pre-installed; and modifications or add-ons that we officially support.

We do not provide support for bespoke modifications, plugins, or configurations built specifically for you or a particular use case, nor for the creation or setup of a service from scratch beyond our standard provisioning. Where a request falls outside what we support, we will, where possible, direct you to suitable independent resources, documentation, or third parties who may be able to assist.

### 2.2 Unmanaged and Self-Managed Services

Other Services provide you with an environment in which you install and manage your own software (for example, virtual private servers and dedicated servers). For these Services, we will generally provide support in relation to: billing; the underlying hardware; any control panel we provide; and the initial installation and basic diagnosis of an operating system that we officially support.

We do not provide support for operating systems that are not on our officially supported list, for operating systems installed or replaced by you outside of our standard provisioning process, or for any configuration that falls outside our standard setup, regardless of whether the underlying operating system is one we officially support. This includes, without limitation, custom images, non-standard partitioning or boot configurations, and modifications made after initial provisioning.

We may, at our discretion and on a reasonable endeavours basis, provide limited assistance with software that we separately host or officially support elsewhere. Assistance with other third-party or unsupported software will be provided only where reasonably practicable, and we give no assurance that we will be able to help. We do not provide support for the installation of software that is unusually complex or time-consuming to configure, for operating system configuration beyond initial setup, or for setting up a service in its entirety on your behalf. Where a request falls outside what we support, we will, where possible, direct you to suitable independent resources, documentation, or third parties who may be able to assist.

### 2.3 General

The above descriptions are indicative and not exhaustive. Whether a particular request falls inside or outside scope, and the extent of any assistance we choose to provide, is determined at our sole discretion. Unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing, matters outside scope also include: data loss, corruption, or recovery beyond any standard backup arrangements expressly included in your Service (see Clause 17 of the Terms — User Data); hardware you own and locate within our facilities under a colocation arrangement, save for matters relating to power, connectivity, or physical access that we control, each of which is instead addressed in our Colocation Agreement; issues arising from third-party providers, networks, or services outside our control, including upstream connectivity, domain registrars, or DNS providers not operated by us; and security incidents or vulnerabilities arising from your own applications, credentials, or configuration.

Where a request is out of scope, we may still choose to assist on a discretionary or chargeable basis; doing so does not create an ongoing obligation to provide the same assistance in future.
