### 4.2 Fair Use

Where a Service is described as "unlimited", "unmetered", or otherwise without a stated numerical limit on a resource (such as bandwidth, storage, or requests), that description is subject to a fair use standard. "Fair use" means resource consumption that is reasonably consistent with the typical, legitimate use of that resource by other customers on a comparable Service tier, and that does not degrade the performance, availability, or security of our infrastructure for other customers.

Whether a particular pattern of use exceeds fair use is determined by us, acting reasonably and having regard to actual usage patterns across comparable customers. Where we consider your use exceeds fair use, we will, where reasonably practicable, contact you first to request that you reduce or adjust your usage before taking further action. We may, in accordance with Clause 7, suspend or restrict the affected Service, require you to move to a different Service tier, or apply reasonable technical measures to manage resource consumption, if usage is not brought within fair use limits.
